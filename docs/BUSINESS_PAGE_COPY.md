## 2. The problem, in one line

People see your work on Instagram and WhatsApp, send "How much?", then disappear. Or they book and don't come.

## 3. A website people book from

**Heading:** A website people book from, free

**Body:** Your page at usetikka.com/yourname: your work, your services and prices, your hours, your reviews. Customers pick a service and a free time, and it's booked. Put the link in your Instagram and WhatsApp bio.

**Points:**
- Only your free times show, so nobody books when you're busy
- Choices like short, medium or long, with their own price and time
- Home visits with your travel fee, and whole days for weddings and shoots
- Six page styles, from soft and light to a photographer's gallery

## 4. Deposits that stop no-shows

**Heading:** No-shows stop costing you

**Body:** Customers pay a small deposit to hold their time. It goes straight to your bank the next working day. If they don't come, the deposit is already yours.

**Points:**
- You pick the deposit: a percentage or a fixed amount, at least ₦1,000
- Your booking rules show before they pay, so everyone knows where they stand
- They can move with 24 hours' notice and keep their deposit; cancelling doesn't refund it
- Send a pay link for a balance or a custom job, from your phone

**Small print:** Tikka takes 1% of each payment, and Paystack takes its fee. We check your ID before payments switch on.

## 5. Today and Money

**Heading:** Know who's coming and what you made

**Body:** Open Tikka in the morning and see today: who's coming, who paid their deposit, what's left to collect. Tap Paid when they pay you cash or transfer. On Sunday, see what came in, what you spent and what you kept.

**Points:**
- Who still owes you, with a reminder you send from your own WhatsApp
- Receipts your customers can keep
- A note at 8pm: anything you spent today?

## 6. Your customers

**Heading:** Every customer, one list

**Body:** Everyone who booked, bought or claimed an offer from you, by their WhatsApp number: their visits, what they spent, your notes and their birthday. When a day is full, they can join your waitlist, and you tell them when a place opens.

**Small print:** Only you see your customers' numbers. Staff who check codes at the counter never do.

## 7. Drops: new customers, when you want them

**Heading:** Bring in new customers with a drop

**Body:** A drop is a limited batch of offers from you: a few big ones, some good ones, and a small thank-you for everyone else. People open one on your link, it lands on their WhatsApp, and they show it at your counter. You get every claimer's number, and you see what the drop brought in, in naira.

**Points:**
- Ready-made offers for your trade, so you don't start from nothing
- One link for your bio and status, and a QR poster for your counter
- On Tikka's homepage under "Offers near you"

## 9. FAQs

1. **Do my customers need an app?** No. They book on your page and get a booking pass. Drop offers arrive in WhatsApp.
2. **How do I get paid?** Add your ID and bank account in Get paid. Once we've checked your ID, deposits and pay links go straight to your bank the next working day. Tikka never holds your money.
3. **Who refunds a deposit?** Tikka doesn't refund payments. If you agree a refund with a customer, send it from your bank. Your booking rules say what happens when someone cancels.
4. **Can I take bookings without deposits?** Yes. Deposits are your choice, for every service or just some.
5. **I work from home, or I go to customers. Does it work?** Yes. Set where you work, your travel fee and how far you go. Customers give their address when they book.
6. **Can my staff use it?** Yes. Invite them to check codes and mark who came. They never see customers' numbers or your money.
7. **Can people in other cities book me?** Yes. Tikka is for businesses anywhere in Nigeria, and customers search by city and area.
8. **What does it cost?** The website, bookings and daily tools are free. Payments cost 1% plus Paystack's fee. Drops are ₦10,000 each after your first free one.
