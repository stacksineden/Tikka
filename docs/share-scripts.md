# Share scripts for new businesses

Ready-to-send words for business owners who just got set up on Tikka and want to tell their people, but don't know what to say.

**How to use:** copy one, fill in the [brackets], and send it to the owner on WhatsApp with a short note (see "Message to the owner" below). Tell them to change any word so it sounds like them. Their own voice works better than ours.

**Rules for anything we write here:** no gambling words (win, winner, luck, lucky, jackpot, odds, bet, stake, lottery, raffle, prize draw), naira is always ₦, and never promise a reward we haven't set up.

---

## Message to the owner (from us)

> Hi [Owner name]! Your page is live: [page link] 🎉
>
> If you want to share it on your status or Instagram, here are a few things you can post. Pick one, change anything you like, and make it sound like you. When people tap your link, they see your services, photos and how to reach you.
>
> We're also running drops for businesses this November, so let your people know something is coming. We'll help you set yours up.

## Tips to pass on to the owner

- **Post a screenshot of your page** with the link. People tap pictures more than plain text.
- **Put the link in your WhatsApp and Instagram bio** so it's always there.
- **Post more than once.** Not everyone sees your first status. Post again in two or three days.
- **Say it in your own words.** These are just a start.
