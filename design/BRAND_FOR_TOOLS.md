# Tikka brand guide (for design and image tools)

Give this file to any image, video or design tool before asking it for Tikka work: teasers, carousels, posts, banners, posters, logo exports. Attach the logo files listed in section 3 with it. The full brand book is `design/BRAND.md`; this is the short version a tool can follow.

---

## Paste this at the top of any prompt

> Brand: Tikka, a Nigerian platform where businesses run "drops" of collectible digital cards (Tikkas) that people claim on WhatsApp and use in store. Idea: "Your ticket to something good."
> Look: deep night indigo backgrounds (#1B1440), paper-white text (#FAF7F2), indigo accent (#3E2BB0), gold (#F4B63F) only on the logo or a reward. Flat, clean, premium, warm. No gradients except a soft indigo glow, no glossy 3D, no stock-photo clichés.
> Type: Bricolage Grotesque (bold, tight, lowercase-friendly) for all text; Space Mono only for small labels, codes and dates.
> Logo: use the attached Tikka logo files exactly as they are. Never redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to them.
> Voice: short, warm, honest. Never use gambling words (win, winner, luck, lucky, jackpot, odds, bet, lottery, raffle, prize). No emoji. Money is always written with ₦ (₦5,000), never N or NGN.

---

## 1. What Tikka is

- **What it does:** Tikka turns people who see a business online into customers who walk in.
- **Drops and Tikkas:** a business runs a **drop**, a limited batch of collectible digital cards called **Tikkas**. Each Tikka holds a reward: **Legendary** (very few), **Rare** (some) or **Thank-you** (everyone else).
- **The customer's side:** people claim a Tikka on WhatsApp, flip it to see what they got, and show it at the counter.
- **Brand idea:** "Your ticket to something good."
- **Personality:** warm, playful, premium, honest. It should feel like being handed a ticket to a good party, never like a betting slip or a cheap coupon.
- **Where:** Nigeria, nationwide. Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan and beyond. Never present it as Lagos-only.
- **Who it's for:** walk-in businesses (salons, barbers, spas, gyms, clinics, food spots, tutors, events) and their customers, mostly on Android phones.
- **Sub-brands:**
  - **Tikka Studio:** our done-for-you service (websites, WhatsApp automations, ads and content).
  - **Spark:** an AI drop strategist, coming soon.

**What the logo looks like, so you can recognise it, not redraw it:**
- **The icon:** a slim portrait ticket tilted about 12° (the top leans left), rounded corners, with a big four-point star ("the spark") on the upper part. A gold stub sits below a dashed perforation, with small half-circle notches on both edges at 72% of the height.
- **Colours on light backgrounds:** an indigo ticket, a gold spark and a gold stub.
- **Colours on dark backgrounds (reversed):** a paper-white ticket, an indigo spark and a gold stub.
- **The name:** "tikka" in heavy lowercase Bricolage Grotesque, with the dot of the "i" replaced by a small gold spark.

**Rules:**
- **Never alter it:** don't redraw, recolour, stretch, squash, untilt, rotate, outline, shadow, glow or bevel the logo.
- **Pick the right version:** on night or any dark background, use the **reversed** files. Never put the logo on a business's brand colour or on a busy photo; over photos use the mono files.
- **Give it space:** keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the gold stub.
- **Minimum sizes:** the icon at least 24px; the lockup at least 24px tall.
- **No second dot:** never show a normal dot on the "i" alongside the spark.

## 4. Colours

Use only these. Night, paper and indigo do most of the work.

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | #1B1440 | Main background for social, teasers and anything premium. The "stage". |
| Night raised | #2A2160 | Panels, pills and cards sitting on night. |
| Paper | #FAF7F2 | Main text on night; light backgrounds. |
| On-night white | #F6F3FF | Text on night (the reversed logo uses this). |
| Soft lilac text | #B3ABD6 | Secondary text on night (dates, small lines). |
| Indigo | #3E2BB0 | Tikka's colour: buttons, highlights, a soft glow behind the logo. |
| Ink | #17112F | Text on light backgrounds. |
| Gold | #F4B63F | **Scarce.** Only the logo's spark and stub, rewards, the Rare tier and the claim button. Never as a background wash or decoration. |
| Lilac (Thank-you tier) | #C9C4E6 | The Thank-you tier on a card. |
| Zobo pink (Legendary tier) | #FF5C8A | The Legendary tier only. |

**Never:**
- **No WhatsApp green** (#25D366) as a Tikka colour.
- **No rainbow gradients or neon.**
- **No business brand colours** on the stage or the stub.

The one gradient allowed is a soft radial **indigo glow** (#3E2BB0 fading to transparent) behind the logo or the hero text on night.

## 5. Typography

- **Bricolage Grotesque** for everything: headlines, body text, buttons. Headlines are bold to extra-bold (700–800), tightly spaced (about -2% to -3%), sentence case. Body is regular to medium.
- **Space Mono** only for small labels: dates ("November 2026"), codes ("GS-7X4QP"), serial numbers ("No. 0412 / 600"), counters ("23 of 600 left"). It reads like a printed ticket.
- Both are free on Google Fonts. Load the latin-ext subset so ₦ renders.
- **Sentence case everywhere:** "Something good is coming." Never Title Case or ALL CAPS headlines. Small Space Mono labels may be uppercase.
- **No other fonts.** Never set "tikka" in another font.

## 6. Shapes and motifs

- **The ticket:** portrait 5:7, rounded corners, a dashed perforation at 72% of the height with half-circle notches cut into both edges, and a stub below. It's Tikka's signature; use it for cards and key panels, not as wallpaper.
- **The spark:** a four-point star with curved sides, from the logo. Tikka's only custom glyph. Use it for rewards and reveals, in gold, sparingly.
- **The card's two sides:**
  - **Front:** shows the business (its colour, logo and name).
  - **Back:** night raised, with the reward and a stub in the tier colour.
  - **Tier pills:** Legendary has 3 dots and zobo pink; Rare has 2 dots and gold; Thank-you has 1 dot and lilac. Always write the word, never colour alone.
- **The sealed pack:** before opening, a Tikka is a sealed indigo pack with crimped edges, "TEAR HERE", the spark and the business's round seal.
- **Corners and depth:** rounded corners; flat colour; one soft shadow under the card only. No glossy 3D, no bevels.
- **Icons:** simple line icons (Lucide style), 1.75px stroke, same colour as the text beside them.

## 7. Imagery

- **Main look:** night stage graphics: night background, soft indigo glow, paper-white type, the ticket and the logo. Clean and spacious, one idea per image.
- **Photos (when used):** real Nigerian businesses and people: salons, barbers, food spots, gyms, markets, friends showing a phone. Natural light, warm skin tones well exposed, candid moments. No stock-photo poses, no Western-only settings.
- **Phones in shots:** use modern Android or iPhone frames, with the Tikka claim screen (night background, the card) on the screen.
- **Avoid:**
  - Anything that looks like gambling: dice, slot machines, coins raining, scratch cards, roulette, trophies.
  - Confetti everywhere, fake luxury (gold bars, champagne), clip art.

## 8. Voice and copy

- **Tone:** short, warm, honest. One idea per line, verbs first. A light Nigerian warmth is welcome in marketing ("Oya, your Tikka is waiting"), never in legal or error text.
- **Lines to reuse:**
  - "Your ticket to something good."
  - "Every Tikka holds something."
  - "You got a Tikka!"
  - "Tear it open."
  - "Claim it on WhatsApp."
  - "Show it at the counter."
  - "Something good is coming."
  - "First drop free."
  - "No app needed."
- **Business lines lead with numbers:** "This drop brought in ₦1.9M." "23 of 600 left."
- **Banned words, anywhere:** win, winner, won, luck, lucky, jackpot, odds, bet, stake, lottery, raffle, prize, prize draw, spin, gamble. Say "you got", "every Tikka holds something", "claim".
- **No hype:** no "Hurry!!!", "Last chance!!!", "Huge ROI".
- **Money:** always ₦ with commas: ₦5,000, ₦25,000, ₦1.9M. Never N, NGN or "naira" after a number.
- **No emoji in images.** WhatsApp messages may open with one ticket emoji, no more.
- **Counts:** "23 of 600 left" (remaining first); serials "No. 0412 / 600".

## 9. Sizes and safe areas

| Format | Size | Keep clear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile photo (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp) | 1080×1080 | Everything inside the central circle, at least 12% in from the edge. |
| X profile photo | 400×400 | As above. |
| Instagram or LinkedIn post, carousel slide | 1080×1350 (or 1080×1080) | 80px margin on all sides; keep the logo in the same corner on every slide. |
| WhatsApp status, Instagram or TikTok story, Reel cover | 1080×1920 | Nothing important in the top 250px or bottom 250px. |
| X header | 1500×500 | Nothing important in the bottom-left 400×200 (the profile photo covers it). |
| Story highlight cover | 1080×1920 | Icon centred, inside the middle circle. |

**Carousels:**
- **Slide 1** is the hook: one bold sentence and the logo.
- **Middle slides:** one idea each.
- **Last slide:** the call to action ("Start free at usetikka.com" or "Claim it on WhatsApp").
- **Consistency:** the same background, margins and logo position on every slide.
