How it works

A QR that never breaks

Each business gets its own card code (usetikka.com/c/GS7KQ). It always opens the business page, even if they change their page link later. The link printed under the QR is the easy one to type.

Scans show up

Visits from the card show as “Business card” in link activity, so owners can see their cards bringing people in.

Says what fits

“Scan to book” when bookings are on, “Scan to see our work” when they’re off. Nothing on the card runs out: no rewards, no prices, no drop.

Their brand, not ours

The card uses the business’s colour, logo and headline font, like their business page. Tikka only shows as a small “Made with Tikka”.
