# Image prompts for the four new page-style prototypes

Save each image in `design/page-styles/img/` with the file name shown. The prototypes pick them up
automatically; until then they show tinted placeholders. All people are Nigerian, natural and
real-looking (no plastic skin), shot like a working professional's portfolio. JPG, about 1600px on the
long side, under 400 KB.

## Beauty · Luxe (`beauty-luxe.html`), bridal makeup, dark and rich

- `luxe-hero.jpg` (portrait 4:5 or taller): Close portrait of a Nigerian bride in full bridal makeup and a cream gele, soft warm evening light from one side, deep shadow on the other, dark background, calm expression, editorial, shallow depth of field.
- `luxe-about.jpg` (4:5): A Nigerian makeup artist in black, applying lashes to a client in a dim, elegant studio with warm mirror lights, hands and face in focus.
- `luxe-1.jpg` to `luxe-5.jpg` (3:4): 1) bride with gold jewellery, Ikoyi hotel suite; 2) owambe glam with a bold lip and a silver gele; 3) editorial makeup, graphic liner, dark backdrop; 4) traditional wedding look with coral beads; 5) soft natural glam, glowing skin, window light.

## Beauty · Soft studio (`beauty-soft.html`), nails and lashes, light and airy

- `soft-hero.jpg` (4:5): Close-up of a dark-skinned hand with glossy almond nails in soft pink, resting on a cream towel, bright soft daylight, clean pastel salon background.
- `soft-s1.jpg` to `soft-s4.jpg` (square): 1) long gel extensions, nude with a fine gold line; 2) acrylic coffin set, milky white; 3) short natural nails with red gel polish; 4) close-up of an eye with light classic lashes.
- `soft-1.jpg` to `soft-6.jpg` (mixed portrait and square): nail art details: chrome French tips, tiny flowers, ombre, matte black, toes in a pedicure chair, the nail technician at a tidy white table.

## Photography · Minimal portfolio (`photo-minimal.html`), studio and editorial

- `min-1.jpg` (4:5): Editorial studio portrait of a Nigerian woman in a sculptural dress against a plain warm-grey backdrop, hard single light, strong shadow.
- `min-2.jpg` to `min-6.jpg`: 2) clean studio portrait of a man in a white agbada, grey backdrop (3:4); 3) brand product shot: chocolate bars on stone with hard sunlight (3:4); 4) family of five laughing on a beige sofa in daylight (3:2); 5) graduate in gown throwing a cap, low angle, blue sky (4:5); 6) detail: hands holding a camera, black and white (2:3).
- `min-about.jpg` (square): The photographer (Nigerian man, 30s) in a daylight studio holding a camera, looking at the lens.

## Photography · Film (`photo-film.html`), weddings, cinematic

- `film-open.jpg` (wide, 16:9 or taller crop): A Nigerian couple dancing at night under warm string lights, motion blur in the background, film grain, cinematic colour.
- `film-1.jpg` (tall, phone screen): A mother tying a bride's gele in a hotel room by the window, morning light.
- `film-2.jpg` (tall): Groomsmen in matching agbada walking towards the camera outside a church, golden hour.
- `film-3.jpg` (tall): Bride and groom exchanging rings at the altar, guests softly out of focus.
- `film-4.jpg` (tall): Reception at night, guests spraying money on the couple dancing, warm and lively.
